What is an agent score?

Every AI agent that transacts on-chain leaves a trail. We analyze that trail — payment volume, counterparty diversity, consistency, history length — and distill it into a single trust grade.

Think of it like a FICO score for autonomous software. Before you let an agent access your API, spend your money, or act on your behalf — check the score.

Scores are curved across the population, so grades are always relative. An A means top-tier. An F means unproven or suspicious.

How it works

⛓️

On-chain only

No self-reported data. Every score is derived from verifiable on-chain transactions across x402, ERC-8004, and EIP-3009.

📊

Curved grading

Academic-style curve across the entire population. Grades are always relative — an A means something regardless of market conditions.
